# Gathering Starlight into a Galaxy, Breaking Monopoly through Coexistence — The Bees-Alliance Common Prosperity Manifesto
![Bees-Alliance](./images/logo.png)

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md) · [한국어](README.ko.md) · [Français](README.fr.md) · [中文](README.md)

In this era, what is lacking is not hard-working people, but **people who unite**.

Every ordinary person, every entrepreneur, every striver who works deeply in their industry, is like a tiny, diligent bee. We work earnestly, contribute quietly, and toil day and night — believing that effort will be rewarded and struggle will bear fruit.

But reality says: **the individual toils, capital harvests; the many create, the few enjoy.**

For too long, monopoly capital has held the channels, the rules, the traffic and the pricing power. It manufactures involution, manufactures competition, manufactures anxiety — making thousands upon thousands of strivers fight and consume one another, so that everyone's sweat, time and creativity ultimately serves only to inflate the wealth of a handful of giants.

We run ourselves ragged, yet cannot hold on to the fruits; we create value collectively, yet cannot share the dividends.

![Life is not easy](./images/life-is-hard.png)

This is not because we don't work hard enough — **the era of going it alone has long since lost all fairness.**

But the wisdom of bees has never been to fight alone.
The greatest strength of bees is **living in colonies, coexisting, co-building and co-sharing.**

Bees-Alliance was born precisely to break this injustice, reshape the industry ecosystem, and achieve common prosperity for ordinary people.

We harvest no capital, we exploit through no hierarchy, we play no game of involution.
What we want to do is **a group of ordinary people, uniting, using collective strength to stand against capital monopoly.**

1. **We reject involutionary competition and build a new order of mutual aid and coexistence**
In the past, capital loved to see us fight over scraps, undercut each other's prices, and suspect one another. The more scattered we are, the more capital controls; the more we involute, the more the giants profit.
Bees-Alliance overturns this rule completely: **internally we help one another, share resources, share experience and enable each other; externally we stand together, speak with one voice, develop jointly and move forward together.**
Let involution vanish, let goodwill return, and let no striver stand alone.

2. **We break capital monopoly and reclaim the voice and earnings that belong to ordinary people**
The essence of capital monopoly is the monopoly of resources, opportunities and profits.
Yet the labor, traffic, market and reputation of countless workers like us is the true foundation of the industry.
What Bees-Alliance will do is **gather scattered tiny forces into a mighty collective power**, replacing capital's centralized power with collective collaboration and oligarchic hoarding with co-building and co-sharing — so that rules return to fairness, resources return to the masses, and profits return to the creators.

3. **We hold fast to wholeheartedly serving our partners, building a community of common prosperity for all**
We always practice the founding aspiration of wholeheartedly serving the people — **we build no giant capital, only a community.**
We have no elevated capital hierarchy, only entrepreneurial partners walking shoulder to shoulder;
No handful reaping huge profits, only **more work, more pay; co-build, co-share; all-member dividends; common prosperity.**
Every contribution is seen, every contribution is rewarded, every value is shared.
Every diligent little bee, so long as they toil, will find nectar; every partner who truly walks with us, so long as they stay the course, will reap rewards.

Walking alone, every step is hard; walking together, every obstacle can be broken.
Capital can monopolize markets, but it can never monopolize **unity, kindness, diligence and coexistence.**

Here, I extend my most sincere and resolute call to all strivers who refuse to be ordinary, who refuse to be harvested, who yearn for fairness and who pursue common prosperity:

**May all fellow travelers who harbor passion, hold true to their founding aspiration and work steadfastly, join Bees-Alliance!**
Let us abandon suspicion, lay down involution, join hands and build together as one.
Take the diligence of bees to root ourselves in the industry, take the strength of the swarm to break monopoly, take the heart of coexistence to distribute dividends.

Together, **we will build the platform, the rules, the business and the wealth community that belong to ordinary people themselves.**
From now on, **labor has dignity, struggle has reward, value is shared, all prosper together!**

Gathered faint lights become a galaxy; countless hearts aligned reach flourishing together.
Let us take the power of the ordinary and create an extraordinary era!
**Everyone creates, everyone shares, everyone prospers, everyone shines!**
